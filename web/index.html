FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is SmartQ free for customers?

Yes. Creating an account, booking a turn and following your queue is completely free for customers.

Do I need a password?

No. Enter your email and we send you a 6-digit code to sign in. You can still use a password if you prefer.

Do I need to install anything?

No. SmartQ runs in your browser on any computer, tablet or phone. An optional Android app is available if you prefer it.

How do I get my queue number?

Pick a place, choose a schedule and a free time slot, then confirm. Your ticket with a number like Q-007 appears instantly and stays in “My appointments”.

I run a clinic / salon. How do I start?

Choose “Register a business” on the sign-up page, add your place details, then create a schedule — SmartQ splits it into bookable slots automatically.

Install the SmartQ Android app

The app is optional — the website does everything. Installing takes about a minute.

  1. Download the APKTap “Download the Android app (APK)” on your Android phone. The file is SmartQ.apk.
  2. Allow “Install unknown apps”When Android asks, open Settings and allow your browser (e.g. Chrome) to install unknown apps. You only do this once.
  3. Open & installOpen the downloaded file from the notification or your Downloads folder and tap Install.
  4. Sign inOpen SmartQ and sign in with your email (we send you a code), or browse as a guest.

Android may warn that the app is not from the Play Store — that is expected for a direct download.
